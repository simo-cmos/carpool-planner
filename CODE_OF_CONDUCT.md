# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the project

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information — including home addresses or location
  data — without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and may remove,
edit, or reject contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

Report abusive or unacceptable behaviour by opening a private report through the
repository's [Security tab](../../security/advisories/new), which is visible only
to maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated.
